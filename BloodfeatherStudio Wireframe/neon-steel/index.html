The World

A city that chews you up and asks for more.

Vektor City, 2087. The megacorps won. The streets belong to gangs, junkies, and mercenaries for hire. You are ARIA — a cybernetically-enhanced operative with a fractured memory and a contract on your own head.

Every alley tells a story. Every corpo enforcer is someone's family. Every choice you make ripples through a world that is always watching, always recording, always ready to use it against you.

⚔

Fluid Combat System

Chain abilities, environmental kills, and real-time tactical decisions in top-down arenas that reward creativity over button mashing.

◈

Branching Narrative

30+ story branches shaped by your choices. Factions remember your actions. The city's fate changes based on who you side with — and who you betray.

⬡

Cybernetic Augmentation

Build ARIA from the chrome up. Skill trees span stealth, brute force, hacking, and persuasion — mix and match for a playstyle no one has seen.

♫

Original Soundtrack

A hand-crafted synth-noir score that shifts dynamically with gameplay — calm in exploration, frantic in combat, haunting in the aftermath.

Act I — The Waking

You wake up with blood on your hands and no memory of whose it is.

ARIA, a black-market operative, surfaces in the lower bowels of Vektor City with a data chip embedded in her skull and a target on her back. The chip holds something the three mega-corporations would kill to erase.

Act II — The Fracture

Factions want you. Factions want you dead. Sometimes the same faction wants both.

Navigate the Ironclad Syndicate, the ghost-network of the Echoes, and the cold efficiency of Helios Corp. Every alliance costs something. Nothing is free in Vektor City.

Act III — The Reckoning

The truth about who made you — and what you were made to do.

The answers ARIA finds may cost the city everything. Multiple endings, none of them clean. This is a world that earns its endings.
