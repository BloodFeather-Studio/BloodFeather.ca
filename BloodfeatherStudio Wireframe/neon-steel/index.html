The World

A city that chews you up and asks for more.

A dystopian futuristic city runs on Neon — a glowing energy source powering its batteries, machinery, and everyday life. You are Kade Ito, a gruff construction worker from the city's impoverished labor districts, whose life has been shaped by hard work, bad conditions, and keeping his head down.

The game begins in the Docks, where Kade is caught in the fallout of his estranged twin brother Ryo's actions. Suddenly forced to run from his own crew, the authorities, and the people controlling the city from above — what starts as a desperate escape becomes a fight to survive, uncover the truth, and reach Ryo before the city closes in around him.

⚔

Expanding Arsenal

Start with hand-to-hand and grow into the sword, gun, and grappling hook — each one changing not just how you attack, but how you move and control a fight. Swap weapons mid-combo to chain devastating sequences on the fly.

◈

Neon Power System

Neon is the city's lifeblood — and Kade's fuel. Build it through aggression and sustained combos, then spend it on shields, special abilities, and a temporary mech state that enhances every weapon and unlocks devastating ultimate attacks.

⬡

Grappling Hook Traversal

Latch onto traversal points, swing across platforms, grab enemies, or hurl light objects as weapons. Destructible environments turn cover and set pieces into part of the action.

✦

Absorbed Enemy Abilities

Defeat major Mantis Enforcers to take their powers and add new situational tools to Kade's arsenal. Every boss you overcome makes you more dangerous than before.

Act I — The Fallout

You didn't do anything wrong. That stopped mattering the moment Ryo did.

Kade Ito is a construction worker. Loyal, stubborn, and invisible — exactly the way the city likes its labor. When his estranged twin brother Ryo's actions catch up with both of them, Kade is suddenly running from his own crew, the authorities, and people he's never even met.

Act II — The Run

Ryo is the voice in your ear — and the reason the city wants you silenced.

Guiding Kade through the Docks and deeper into the city, Ryo becomes the thread connecting everything: why the city's ruling powers want the brothers silenced, and what Neon is really tied to. The story stays grounded in the working class side of this world — exploitation, survival, and the cost of staying alive somewhere built to grind you down.

Act III — The Truth

Reach Ryo before the city closes in around you.

What starts as a desperate escape becomes a fight to survive and uncover the truth. Kade is not a chosen hero or a trained soldier — he is a man who refused to disappear. That makes the hits harder, and the answers more costly.
