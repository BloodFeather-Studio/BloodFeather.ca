FAQ

Answers to questions we get a lot.

Can I playtest Neon Steel?

We're not running a public playtest yet, but we do occasional closed sessions. Send us a message with "Playtest" in the subject and we'll add you to the list.

Are you hiring or looking for collaborators?

Not currently, but we love meeting talented people. If you're a musician, animator, writer, or developer who vibes with our work, reach out. The future is long.

When does Neon Steel release?

Neon Steel releases June 30, 2026 on PC. Follow us on socials or drop your email in the form to get notified the moment it's live.

Can I write about or cover BloodFeather Studio?

Please do. We have a press kit available on request — just drop us a message with "Press" in the subject line and we'll get it to you within 24 hours.
