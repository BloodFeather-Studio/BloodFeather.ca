About the Studio

Four students. One shared obsession with dark worlds and the stories that haunt them.

Our Origin

Born in the dark hours of a capstone semester.

BloodFeather Studio didn't begin with a business plan or a pitch deck. It began with a shared conviction: that games could be more than entertainment — they could be literature. Art. An experience that leaves a mark.

We met in our final year of game design, four students from different disciplines, united by an appetite for atmosphere and a stubborn refusal to make something forgettable. The name came late one night — blood for sacrifice, feather for craft. Two things every creative endeavour demands.

Neon Steel is our first title and our statement of intent. It won't be our last.

What We Stand For

The principles behind every decision.

These aren't poster-words. They're the constraints we work within — the promises we make to the player before they even pick up the controller.

01

Story Above All

Every mechanic, every system, every pixel exists to serve the narrative. If a feature doesn't deepen the story or the world, it doesn't ship.

02

Atmosphere is Design

The feeling of a space — its light, its sound, its texture — is as carefully designed as any combat system. You don't just play our worlds; you inhabit them.

03

Respect the Player

No hand-holding. No artificial difficulty. We design for players who want to think, explore, and feel the weight of their choices without being babied through it.

04

Details Are Everything

The things players notice consciously are 10% of what shapes their experience. The other 90% is everything we obsessed over that they'll never be able to name but always feel.

05

Dark Doesn't Mean Nihilistic

Our worlds are dark because the world is. But darkness that earns its light — that's what we're after. Something that cuts, but also illuminates.

06

Collaboration Over Ego

The best idea wins, regardless of who had it. We leave our titles at the door and build together, because no single person's vision is as rich as four minds in conversation.

Our Journey

How we got here.

Sep 2024

The Studio Forms

Four students from different disciplines agree on one thing: the capstone project should be something that actually matters. BloodFeather Studio is named on a napkin.

Oct 2024

Concept Greenlit

After three rejected pitches (one fantasy, one sci-fi horror, one we don't talk about), Neon Steel finds its footing: cyberpunk noir, top-down, narrative-first.

Nov 2024

Pre-Production Begins

World-building documents, design pillars, and the first sketches of Vektor City. The art direction takes shape. Jordan builds the first prototype in a weekend.

Jan 2025

Alpha Build

First playable build. It's rough, it crashes constantly, and we play it for six hours straight anyway. We know we're onto something.

Mar 2025

Beta Milestone

Full Act I complete with branching dialogue. Taylor's soundtrack enters the build for the first time. The game finally feels like a game.

May 2025

Capstone Presentation

BloodFeather Studio presents Neon Steel to faculty, peers, and industry judges. This site is part of that presentation. We're not done — we're just getting started.
